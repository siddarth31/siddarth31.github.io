<h3><strong>A Star's Final Act</strong></h3> 

<p> NGC 6992, the Eastern Veil Nebula, is a vast supernova remnant located in the constellation Cygnus, approximately 2,500 light-years from Earth. </p> 
<p> The Veil Nebula formed when a massive star reached the end of its life in a catastrophic supernova between 5,000 and 10,000 years ago. The immense energy released sent shockwaves racing through the surrounding interstellar medium. </p> <p> The striking colors revealed in the astrophotography arise from different elements energized by the expanding shockwave. The red hues are produced primarily by glowing hydrogen, while the blue and cyan tones originate from oxygen emissions. </p>
